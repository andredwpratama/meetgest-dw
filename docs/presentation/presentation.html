What it does & why

Every day the team runs client briefs, campaign reviews and syncs. Afterwards someone is supposed to write up notes and post action items, but it rarely happens consistently. Notes get messy, tasks get forgotten, and there's no single place to see what was decided.

Why we built this Meeting notes were inconsistent and action items often got lost in chat threads. MeetGest standardizes the write-up and makes ownership clear, so decisions and follow-ups don't slip.
The 30-second catch-up. Anyone who missed a meeting can read the summary, see who owns what, and know exactly what was decided, without watching a recording or chasing people.
